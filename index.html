✦ M.SC. THESISOCT 2026

Temporal Semantic Graph Learning for Early Depression Risk Detection from Social Media User Timelines

MWSC study · Temporal graph learning for mental health risk detection

  • Built temporal-semantic graphs from 782,384 writings by 1,600 eRisk-derived users using frozen ModernBERT embeddings.
  • Compared GCN, GraphSAGE, GAT, GATv2, GINE, and TransformerConv against TF–IDF and Transformer–BiLSTM baselines; assessed temporal and feature fusion.
  • GATv2 achieved 0.7592 Macro-F1 and 0.8705 ROC-AUC on 240 held-out users; conducted bootstrap analysis and paired McNemar tests.
  • Rebuilt graphs per chunk without future posts. Time-aware fusion detected 23 of 32 positive users at threshold 0.50, with a mean first alert at chunk 2.30 of 10 among those detected.

Leveraging Web Scraping and Stacking Ensemble Machine Learning Techniques to Enhance Detection of Major Depressive Disorder from Social Media Posts

Social Network Analysis and Mining · Volume 14, Article 239

First-author research developing a Reddit-based NLP pipeline and stacking ensemble approach. The study analyzed more than 45,000 posts and reported 96.47% classification accuracy on the collected dataset.
